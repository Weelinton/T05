Key Takeaways

It can be observed that some LCD(LED) models had the lowest star ratings and the highest energy consumption. Overall, many of the models, including models that uses LCD(LED), are clustered around 3.5-6 stars and consumes less than 1,400 kWh of energy per year. OLED has the highest mean energy consumption in both comparisons, with all screen sizes and among 55-inch TVs.
